About Zenith Dental

We're Redefining the Dental Experience

At Zenith Dental, we believe a visit to the dentist should be as comfortable as visiting a friend. Founded in 2010 by Dr. Sarah Mitchell, our clinic focuses on minimally invasive dentistry and preventive care.

Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with 3D imaging, noiseless drills, and entertainment screens in every ceiling to ensure you stay relaxed while we perfect your smile.
